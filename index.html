HOW TO PLAY

Connect Your Wallet

Use the connect button and choose a supported Solana wallet before making swaps.

Accept Fares

Tap passengers on the map to review their route, reward, patience timer, and swap requirement.

Swap For Fuel

Complete swaps to earn fuel. Higher-value swaps can keep the tuk moving longer.

Finish The Ride

Drive from pickup to destination, avoid stalling, and collect points when the passenger arrives.
