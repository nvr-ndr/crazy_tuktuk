HOW TO PLAY

🛺 Choose a passenger

Tap a passenger marker to see their swap requirement, reward, and the fuel needed to reach them.

⚡ Swap and pick up

Complete a qualifying DFlow swap to earn fuel and activate the selected fare.

🗺️ Drive across Bangkok

Follow the road route to the destination. Your tuk-tuk spends fuel as it moves.

⛽ Avoid a stall

If fuel reaches zero, the trip stops at that point. Swap again to refuel, finish the fare, and earn points.
